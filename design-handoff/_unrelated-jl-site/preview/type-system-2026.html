This is not a mindfulness program. It's what I actually used — on a radiation table, in the silence after everyone went home and I was sitting alone with all of it.

I'm not writing from the other side of that experience. I'm writing from inside it. Short, declarative sentences. Em dashes for rhythm. Fragments used intentionally. Paragraph breaks are generous — white space is part of the message.
