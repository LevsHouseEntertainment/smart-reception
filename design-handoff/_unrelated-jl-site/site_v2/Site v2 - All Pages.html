02 / The full story

I was 35. At my desk in Nashville. Then there was a seizure.

Five doctors walked into my ER room. The scan showed a mass. Within days I was in surgery. Then chemotherapy. Then 33 sessions of radiation, each one strapped to a table by a mesh mask molded to my face.

I had been studying mind-body tools for years. Quiet study — books, breathwork, meditation, a curiosity about why some people walk through impossible things and stay themselves. Suddenly the study wasn't theoretical.

I built a practice from what worked. Three steps. Affirm. Visualize. Feel. I used it on the radiation table. I used it in the silence after everyone went home. I used it when fear got loud — which was often.

I wrote the book during treatment. You Are Stronger Than You THINK. Not from the other side of the experience. From inside it. That matters. The reader gets to make the story about themselves.

I'm still in active treatment. Still using the practice. Still beautifully imperfect. Grateful for all of it.

03 / The framework

AVF — Affirm. Visualize. Feel.

A

Affirm

Speak it. Specifically. Out loud or in your head — whatever the moment allows. Not generic positivity. Words that match what you actually need to hear.

V

Visualize

See it. Hold the image. Your body responds to the picture in your mind before it responds to the room.

F

Feel

Drop into the feeling already. Not pretending — practicing. The feeling is the destination and the path at the same time.
