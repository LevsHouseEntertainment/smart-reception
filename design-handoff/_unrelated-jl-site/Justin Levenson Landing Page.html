The short version

I was at my desk in Nashville when I had a seizure. Five doctors walked into my ER room.

Brain cancer. Brain surgery. Fifteen months of chemotherapy and radiation. I was 35. I had been studying mind-body tools for years — and suddenly they weren't theoretical anymore. They were the difference between surviving and not.

I'm not writing from the other side of that experience. I'm writing from inside it. The practices in this guide are exactly what I did — on the table, at home, in the fear and the quiet and the long days of treatment.

They work. I want to share them with you.
