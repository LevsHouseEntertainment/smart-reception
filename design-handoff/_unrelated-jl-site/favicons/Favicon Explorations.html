Favicon · Light Directions

Six lighter favicon directions.

Six takes on the JL monogram favicon, pulled from the brand palette. All use the existing interlocked-JL mark (Asset 15, outlined version — it holds up best at 16×16). Each card shows 64, 32, and 16px previews plus a browser-tab mock so you can feel which reads cleanest in real life.

Option 01

Linen on Deep Sea

Brand off-white ground, Deep Sea ink. The "default" light version — warm but anchored.

My picks, service-first: — 01 (Linen on Deep Sea) is the safest, most on-brand daily driver. — 05 (Slate on White) reads lightest without feeling empty. — 06 (Circle Seal) is the most recognizable at 16px; it has the most "identity". The gold version is beautiful but louder — good for social avatars, possibly too warm for a tab.
